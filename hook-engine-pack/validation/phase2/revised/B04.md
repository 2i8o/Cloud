# B04 - Myopia-control lenses for parents  (v1.1.0)

**Brief:** Educate parents about myopia progression options. No brands or clinics.  
**Audience:** Iraqi parents of children 6-12 | **Platform:** YouTube Short | **Duration:** 40 s

**Idea verdict:** PROCEED with MANDATORY professional review (paediatric treatment). Confidence: مرجح.

**Recommendation:** B - corrects a harmful habit with sourced evidence and a clear action

## Finalist A - M18 / `lenses-average-effect` / payoff `what-slowing-means`

- **Hook:** ٢٣ دراسة على ١٣ ألف طفل… شنو يعني "تبطّئ" قصر النظر؟
- **Gloss:** 23 trials on 13k children... what does 'slows' myopia mean?
- **Curiosity gap:** What does 'slow' mean for my child? (carried by: voice)
- **First frame:** Number counter, then an animated eye growing slightly longer with and without special lenses
- **Continuation:** Average effect is modest and design-dependent
- **Payoff:** Quantified meaning of 'slow' and what it is not
- **Retention plan:**

| time (s) | purpose | new information | draft spoken line |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0-6 | hook | numbers raise a question | ٢٣ دراسة على ١٣ ألف طفل… شنو يعني "تبطّئ" قصر النظر؟ |
| 6-14 | what myopia progression is | the eye grows longer, power increases | قصر النظر يتقدم لما العين تطول. |
| 14-24 | quantified | on average axial length growth is ~0.15 mm less and refraction ~0.31 D less than with single-vision lenses | بالمتوسط العين تطول أقل بحوالي ٠.١٥ ملم، وتقدّم القوة أقل بحوالي ٠.٣ دايوبتر. |
| 24-33 | limits | slowing, not stopping; designs differ; individual response varies | هذا تبطئة مو إيقاف، وتختلف حسب التصميم والطفل. |
| 33-40 | close | decision belongs to the child's eye professional | القرار مع مختص يفحص طفلك. |

- **Evidence record:**
  - Specialty lenses slowed progression on average in 23 RCTs - [S39, verified] quote: "23 RCTs with a total of 13.315 subjects"; scope: children and adolescents; pooled vs single-vision lenses; AL -0.15 mm, SER -0.31 D; design-specific
- **Risk:** Paediatric advice; review required
- **Confidence:** مرجح

## Finalist B - M02 / `undercorrection-myth` / payoff `use-the-full-prescription`

- **Hook:** تقليل قوة نظارة الطفل ما يبطّئ قصر النظر… وممكن يسرّعه.
- **Gloss:** Under-correcting a child's glasses doesn't slow myopia and may speed it.
- **Curiosity gap:** Is that really true? (carried by: text)
- **First frame:** Parent adjusting child's glasses (staged) with `أقل قوة = أبطأ؟` on screen
- **Continuation:** RCT evidence
- **Payoff:** Why full correction is advised
- **Retention plan:**

| time (s) | purpose | new information | draft spoken line |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0-4 | hook | poses the question: Is that really true? (carried by text) | تقليل قوة نظارة الطفل ما يبطّئ قصر النظر… وممكن يسرّعه. |
| 4-12 | why parents try it | belief that weaker lenses 'rest' the eye | كثير أهل يفكرون إن النظارة الأضعف ترتاح بيها العين. |
| 12-24 | what trials found | binocular undercorrection did not slow progression and may accelerate it | التجارب لقت إنها ما تبطّئ، وبعضها لقى تسارع. |
| 24-33 | what is different | specialty lenses are designed differently; they are not 'weaker glasses' | عدسات التحكم بقصر النظر مصممة بشكل ثاني، مو نظارة أضعف. |
| 33-40 | close | use the prescribed power and ask about options | استعمل القوة المكتوبة، واسأل المختص عن الخيارات. |

- **Evidence record:**
  - Binocular undercorrection does not slow myopia progression and may accelerate it - [S41, verified] quote: "undercorrection does not slow myopia progression; instead, it may even accelerate the progression"; scope: review of RCTs; children; one paired-eye monovision study showed the opposite for the undercorrected eye
- **Risk:** Review excerpt only (primary RCTs not opened)
- **Confidence:** مرجح

## Finalist C - M08 / `outdoor-time` / payoff `outdoor-time-evidence`

- **Hook:** وقت العطلة برا البيت… هل يغيّر قصر النظر عند الطفل؟
- **Gloss:** Holiday time outdoors... does it change a child's myopia?
- **Curiosity gap:** Does outdoor time really matter? (carried by: voice)
- **First frame:** Children on a school playground in bright light
- **Continuation:** Seven RCTs in China
- **Payoff:** Evidence and its limits
- **Retention plan:**

| time (s) | purpose | new information | draft spoken line |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0-4 | hook | poses the question: Does outdoor time really matter? (carried by voice) | وقت العطلة برا البيت… هل يغيّر قصر النظر عند الطفل؟ |
| 4-14 | evidence | 7 randomized trials, 9,437 children: lower incidence of myopia | ٧ تجارب عشوائية على أكثر من ٩٠٠٠ طفل: اقل ظهور لقصر النظر. |
| 14-26 | limits | all trials in China; dose unclear; mixed evidence once a child is already myopic | كلها بالصين، وما نعرف الجرعة، والأثر على اللي عنده قصر نظر أضعف. |
| 26-34 | safety | sun protection matters | وانتبه للشمس: قبعة ونظارة بحماية UV. |
| 34-40 | close | one realistic target | جرّب تزيد وقت اللعب برا بشكل منتظم. |

- **Evidence record:**
  - More outdoor time lowered myopia incidence in children in 7 RCTs - [S40, verified] quote: "seven randomized controlled trials involving a total of 9,437 subjects"; scope: children; all trials conducted in China; RR 0.84 for incidence
  - Outdoor-time effect on children who are already myopic is mixed - [S41, verified] quote: "Whether the protective effect ... extends to those who are already myopic is equivocal"; scope: review
- **Risk:** Iraqi heat/UV context not covered by the trials
- **Confidence:** مرجح
