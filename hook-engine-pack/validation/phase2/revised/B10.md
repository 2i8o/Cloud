# B10 - Phoropter: one or two?  (v1.1.0)

**Brief:** Explain why the test asks 'which is better, one or two?' and reassure viewers who are unsure.  
**Audience:** Iraqi adults anxious about eye tests | **Platform:** YouTube Short | **Duration:** 30 s

**Idea verdict:** PROCEED (practitioner sources S81-S83, labelled). Confidence: تخمين/مرجح.

**Recommendation:** A - reassurance with an honest allowed answer ('about the same')

## Finalist A - M13 / `unsure-is-expected` / payoff `neither-is-crystal-clear`

- **Hook:** "واحد لو اثنين؟" وانت مو متأكد؟ هذا متوقع.
- **Gloss:** "One or two?" and you're not sure? That's expected.
- **Curiosity gap:** Is it okay to be unsure? (carried by: voice)
- **First frame:** Phoropter close-up, lenses flipping; viewer POV
- **Continuation:** Examiner script
- **Payoff:** Why unsure happens and what to answer
- **Retention plan:**

| time (s) | purpose | new information | draft spoken line |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0-4 | hook | poses the question: Is it okay to be unsure? (carried by voice) | "واحد لو اثنين؟" وانت مو متأكد؟ هذا متوقع. |
| 4-12 | clinician reports | patients often can't decide which is better | كثير مرضى ما يگدرون يقررون. |
| 12-20 | what examiners say | neither choice will be crystal clear | الفاحص يگولك: ولا وحدة راح تكون صافية تماماً. |
| 20-27 | allowed answer | 'about the same' is acceptable | وإذا نفسهم، گول: نفس الشي. |
| 27-30 | close | the viewer's job is an honest comparison and 'about the same' is a valid answer | جاوب بصدق، والباقي على الفاحص. |

- **Evidence record:**

- **Confidence:** تخمين

## Finalist B - M05 / `jcc-step` / payoff `why-the-choices-are-exaggerated`

- **Hook:** بفحص الاستجماتزم، الفرق بين "واحد" و"اثنين" أكبر مما تحس.
- **Gloss:** In astigmatism testing the difference between 'one' and 'two' is bigger than you feel.
- **Curiosity gap:** If it feels small, why is it large? (carried by: text)
- **First frame:** Handheld Jackson cross-cylinder flipping in front of a lens; on-screen text `ليش؟`
- **Continuation:** JCC mechanism
- **Payoff:** A mechanism that explains the odd question
- **Retention plan:**

| time (s) | purpose | new information | draft spoken line |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0-5 | hook | poses the question: If it feels small, why is it large? (carried by voice) | بفحص الاستجماتزم، الفرق بين "واحد" و"اثنين" أكبر مما تحس. |
| 5-13 | tool | a Jackson cross-cylinder flips two lens versions | أداة اسمها Jackson cross-cylinder تقلب بين خيارين. |
| 13-22 | numbers | a +0.25 D cross-cylinder gives a whole-diopter difference between the choices | فرق ٠.٢٥ بالأداة يعطي فرق دايوبتر كامل بين الخيارين. |
| 22-27 | why | a big enough difference lets you compare without moving focus off the retina | حتى تگدر تقارن بدون ما يطلع التركيز عن الشبكية. |
| 27-30 | close | this is one step among several | وهذي خطوة وحدة من خطوات. |

- **Evidence record:**
  - A traditional +0.25 D JCC gives a whole-diopter difference between the two choices - [S82, verified] quote: "A traditional +0.25 D JCC actually gives a whole diopter difference"; scope: trade article 2008; Jackson cross-cylinder step; astigmatism refinement
- **Risk:** Technical for laypeople
- **Confidence:** تخمين

## Finalist C - M23 / `small-differences` / payoff `just-noticeable-difference`

- **Hook:** شوف الصورتين… أي أوضح؟ (مو فحص — بس تحس الإحساس).
- **Gloss:** Look at the two images - which is clearer? (not a test - just the feeling).
- **Curiosity gap:** Can I tell them apart? (carried by: frame)
- **First frame:** Two slightly different blurred lines labelled 1 and 2
- **Continuation:** JND
- **Payoff:** Why small differences are hard
- **Retention plan:**

| time (s) | purpose | new information | draft spoken line |
