# B06 - Blind spot  (v1.1.0)

**Brief:** Interactive demo: viewer finds their own blind spot. Include why we do not notice it.  
**Audience:** Iraqi 16-45, curious | **Platform:** TikTok | **Duration:** 30 s

**Idea verdict:** PROCEED (S69-S71); mechanism hedged because filling-in is debated. Confidence: مرجح.

**Recommendation:** A - no screen geometry needed; sourced finger demo

## Finalist A - M10 / `blind-spot-finger-demo` / payoff `where-the-blind-spot-is`

- **Hook:** غمّض عينك اليسار، مدّ إيدك اليمين، وطالع بظفر إبهامك… خنصرك راح يختفي.
- **Gloss:** Close your left eye, extend your right arm, look at your thumbnail... your little finger will vanish.
- **Curiosity gap:** Why does it vanish? (carried by: voice)
- **First frame:** Close-up of a hand with spread fingers; the pinky wiggles and fades out of focus (illustrative)
- **Continuation:** Optic nerve head has no photoreceptors
- **Payoff:** Where it is and why it is there
- **Retention plan:**

| time (s) | purpose | new information | draft spoken line |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0-5 | hook + instruction | viewer does the steps | غمّض عينك اليسار، مدّ إيدك اليمين، وطالع بظفر إبهامك… خنصرك راح يختفي. |
| 5-12 | do it | spread fingers, wiggle the little finger | فرّد أصابعك وحرّك الخنصر… شفته يختفي؟ |
| 12-20 | why | the optic nerve leaves the retina there; no photoreceptors | هناك العصب البصري يطلع من الشبكية، وماكو خلايا تشوف. |
| 20-26 | size | about 6 degrees across - room for a dozen moons side by side | حجمه حوالي ٦ درجات — يكفي لـ١٢ قمر بجنب بعض. |
| 26-30 | close | this is normal anatomy; it differs from a disease finding | هذا تشريح طبيعي موجود عند الكل. |

- **Evidence record:**
  - Little finger vanishes at the blind spot when the other eye is closed - [S69, verified] quote: "Now wiggle your little finger. It will fall on your blind spot"; scope: left eye closed, right arm extended, gaze on thumbnail; Anstis 2010 primer
- **Risk:** Geometry works for the right eye with left closed; reverse for left
- **Confidence:** مرجح

## Finalist B - M04 / `brain-fills-in` / payoff `brain-fills-in-the-gap`

- **Hook:** بكل عين اكو بقعة بدون خلايا مستقبلة… ليش ما تشوفها؟
- **Gloss:** Each eye has a patch without receptors... why don't you see it?
- **Curiosity gap:** Why is there no visible hole? (carried by: voice)
- **First frame:** A striped pattern with a gap that visually closes (illustration)
- **Continuation:** Perceptual filling-in; debated mechanism
- **Payoff:** What filling-in is and is not known
- **Retention plan:**

| time (s) | purpose | new information | draft spoken line |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0-3 | hook | poses the question: Why is there no visible hole? (carried by voice) | بكل عين اكو بقعة بدون خلايا مستقبلة… ليش ما تشوفها؟ |
| 3-11 | fact | each eye has a spot without photoreceptors | بكل عين اكو بقعة بدون خلايا مستقبلة. |
| 11-19 | filling-in | the area looks filled with the surrounding colour or texture | الدماغ يعبّيها بلون وملمس اللي حواليها. |
| 19-25 | debate | how exactly is debated | وكيف يسويها بالضبط لسه نقاش علمي. |
| 25-30 | close with action | gaps from retinal problems can also be filled in, so routine eye exams matter | وحتى فجوات المرض ممكن تنملأ، لهذا الفحص الدوري مهم. |

- **Evidence record:**
  - The blind spot is a retinal region devoid of photoreceptors - [S69, verified] quote: "a retinal region devoid of photoreceptors"; scope: Anstis 2010 primer
  - Scotomas from retinal damage or glaucoma are also filled in, so patients may not notice - [S69, verified, script-only] quote: "a scotoma is also perceptually filled-in, so effectively that the patient is often not aware"; scope: primer statement; closing beat only, attributed
- **Risk:** The closing health line must stay attributed and action-oriented
- **Confidence:** مرجح

- **Curiosity gap:** How can something that big go unnoticed? (carried by: frame)
- **First frame:** 12 moons lined up across a circle on a night-sky photograph (illustration)
- **Continuation:** Size comparison
- **Payoff:** Scale of the blind spot
- **Retention plan:**
