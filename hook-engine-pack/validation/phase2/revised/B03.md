# B03 - Blue-light glasses  (v1.1.0)

**Brief:** Evidence-based take on blue-light glasses; no brands.  
**Audience:** Iraqi 20-40 considering glasses | **Platform:** TikTok | **Duration:** 30 s

**Idea verdict:** PROCEED. Angle: why the evidence is hard to trust (masking problem), not just 'AAO says no'. Confidence: مرجح.

**Recommendation:** A - original angle with a visual (clear vs yellow lens) and an accurate, hedged payoff

## Finalist A - M20 / `blinding-problem` / payoff `why-trials-are-weak`

- **Hook:** شلون تسوي تجربة على نظارة الضوء الأزرق… واللابس يعرف إنها الحقيقية؟
- **Gloss:** How do you run a trial of blue-light glasses when the wearer knows they are the real ones?
- **Curiosity gap:** Why is it hard to test them fairly? (carried by: voice)
- **First frame:** A clear lens beside a yellow-tinted lens held to the camera
- **Continuation:** Yellow tint prevents masking
- **Payoff:** Why the evidence is weak without saying they are useless
- **Retention plan:**

| time (s) | purpose | new information | draft spoken line |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0-5 | hook + visual | clear vs yellow lens | شلون تسوي تجربة على نظارة الضوء الأزرق… واللابس يعرف إنها الحقيقية؟ |
| 5-12 | the problem | these lenses are yellow, so participants can tell which pair they got | لأنها صفرة، المشارك يعرف أي نظارة لبس. |
| 12-19 | what else limits studies | studies are small and short | والدراسات صغيرة وقصيرة. |
| 19-26 | what is concluded | a 2023 review found no consistent evidence of benefit for eye strain; AAO does not recommend special eyewear for screens | مراجعة ٢٠٢٣ ما لقت دليل ثابت، والأكاديمية ما توصي بيها. |
| 26-30 | close | absence of strong evidence is not proof of harm; habits help | مو معناها مضرّة، معناها الدليل ضعيف. والعادات أوضح فايدة. |

- **Evidence record:**
  - Blue-blocking lens studies used yellow-tinted lenses, limiting masking - [S34, verified] quote: "all of these studies have used yellow-tinted blue-blocking lenses, limiting the adequate masking"; scope: 2023 narrative review citing Lawrenson et al.; small (<50) short studies
  - No consistent evidence supports blue-blocking spectacle lenses for eye strain - [S34, verified, script-only] quote: "there is no consistent evidence to support the use of blue-blocking filters in spectacle lenses"; scope: 2023 narrative review (not systematic)
- **Risk:** Long hook (13 content words); needs a read-aloud trim
- **Confidence:** مرجح

## Finalist B - M16 / `sleep-timing-not-glasses` / payoff `screen-timing-for-sleep`

- **Hook:** قبل النوم، المشكلة مو نظارتك. هي ساعات الشاشة.
- **Gloss:** Before bed the issue isn't your glasses - it's screen hours.
- **Curiosity gap:** What should I do for sleep then? (carried by: text)
- **First frame:** On-screen text: `نظارة ← نوم؟` crossed out, then a clock
- **Continuation:** AAO advice on timing
- **Payoff:** Practical sleep advice with attribution
- **Retention plan:**

| time (s) | purpose | new information | draft spoken line |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0-4 | hook | reframes the cause | قبل النوم، المشكلة مو نظارتك. هي ساعات الشاشة. |
| 4-12 | evidence | some evidence that blue light affects the sleep-wake cycle (AAO) | الأكاديمية تگول اكو دليل إن الضوء يأثر على دورة النوم. |
| 12-20 | advice | avoid screens two to three hours before bed; use night mode | ابتعد عن الشاشة ٢-٣ ساعات گبل النوم، وفعّل الوضع الليلي. |
| 20-26 | why not glasses | AAO says sleep can be improved without special glasses | ما تحتاج نظارة خاصة حسب الأكاديمية. |
| 26-30 | close | a realistic nightly plan the viewer can try tonight | جرّب الليلة تبعد الشاشة، وشوف نومك. |

- **Evidence record:**
  - AAO advises avoiding screens two to three hours before bed - [S33, verified] quote: "avoid using screens two to three hours before bed"; scope: AAO 2021; adults; 2-3 hours
- **Risk:** Sleep is adjacent to the brief; kept because glasses are marketed for sleep
- **Confidence:** مرجح

## Finalist C - M08 / `no-retinal-link` / payoff `no-meaningful-link-to-retina`

- **Hook:** الضوء الأزرق من شاشتك يضر شبكيتك؟ الدليل لحد هسه ما يقول هيچ.
- **Gloss:** Does your screen's blue light harm your retina? The evidence so far doesn't say so.
- **Curiosity gap:** What does the evidence say about retinal damage? (carried by: voice)
- **First frame:** Phone beside a retina diagram with a question mark
- **Continuation:** Lab studies did not mimic screens
- **Payoff:** What is and isn't known
- **Answer is in the hook; beat-2 question:** If lab studies showed damage, why don't they apply to screens?
- **Retention plan:**

| time (s) | purpose | new information | draft spoken line |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0-4 | hook | asks and answers carefully | الضوء الأزرق من شاشتك يضر شبكيتك؟ الدليل لحد هسه ما يقول هيچ. |
| 4-12 | why people worry | lab and animal studies showed cell damage in dishes | التجارب بالأطباق والحيوانات شافت ضرر. |
| 12-19 | why it doesn't transfer | those studies did not mimic screen exposure | بس ما قلّدت ضوء الشاشات الحقيقي. |
| 19-26 | unknowns | long-term cumulative effects are unknown | والأثر الطويل المدى لسه مجهول. |
| 26-30 | close | AAO links overexposure to sun blue/UV light to eye-disease risk | الأكاديمية تربط التعرض الزايد لضوء الشمس بأمراض العين. |

- **Evidence record:**
  - So far the evidence shows no meaningful link between blue light and damage to human retinas - [S32, verified] quote: "the evidence shows no meaningful link between blue light and: damage to human retinas"; scope: AAO Dec 2025; screens; lab/animal studies did not mimic screens
  - Overexposure to blue light and UV from the sun can raise eye-disease risk - [S32, verified, script-only] quote: "overexposure to blue light and UV light rays from the sun can raise the risk of eye disease"; scope: AAO Dec 2025; sun exposure
- **Risk:** Last line 'الشمس أخطر' compares two exposures not directly measured in the source; soften or source before publishing
- **Confidence:** مرجح
