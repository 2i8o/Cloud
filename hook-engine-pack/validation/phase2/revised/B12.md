## Finalist A - M01 / `dark-without-uv` / payoff `tint-is-not-protection`

- **Hook:** نظارة شمس غامقة بدون فلتر UV… شنو ممكن يصير ببؤبؤك؟
- **Gloss:** Dark sunglasses without a UV filter - what may happen to your pupil?
- **Curiosity gap:** What happens to the pupil behind dark lenses with no UV filter? (carried by: voice)
- **First frame:** Dark lens next to a clear lens, both held to the sun (illustrative)
- **Continuation:** Pupil dilates
- **Payoff:** Label vs tint
- **Retention plan:**

| time (s) | purpose | new information | draft spoken line |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0-4 | hook | poses the question: More than what? (carried by voice) | نظارة شمس غامقة بدون فلتر UV… شنو ممكن يصير ببؤبؤك؟ |
| 4-12 | logic | darker view -> pupil stays larger | النظر الغامق يخلي البؤبؤ أكبر. |
| 12-21 | consequence | if UV is not filtered, more unfiltered UV can enter | وإذا ماكو فلتر، يدخل UV أكثر. |
| 21-27 | what to check | the label says 100% UV or UV400 | دوّر على 'UV400' أو '100% UV'. |

- **Evidence record:**
  - Without a UV filter, pupils may stay larger behind dark lenses, letting more unfiltered UV enter - [S87, verified] quote: "because your pupils may remain larger, thus allowing more unfiltered UV rays to enter"; scope: AAO Q&A 2021; single ophthalmologist answer; 'may'
- **Risk:** Comparator not stated in the source; hook is a question and the video says 'may'
- **Confidence:** مرجح

- **Hook:** شنو الملصق الي يخلي نظارتك تحمي فعلاً؟
- **Gloss:** Which label makes your sunglasses actually protect?
- **Curiosity gap:** Which label? (carried by: frame)
- **First frame:** Close-up of a lens sticker with the code blurred, then revealed
- **Continuation:** Standards
- **Payoff:** How to read the protection claim
- **Retention plan:**

| time (s) | purpose | new information | draft spoken line |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0-3 | hook | poses the question: Which label? (carried by frame) | شنو الملصق الي يخلي نظارتك تحمي فعلاً؟ |
| 3-10 | reveal | UV400 or 100% UV protection | UV400 أو 100% UV. |
| 10-19 | what it means | 100% UV: transmittance below 0.05% up to 380 nm | يعني السماحية أقل من ٠.٠٥٪ لحد ٣٨٠ نانومتر. |
| 19-26 | not tint, not price | protection depends on material and coating, not darkness or price | الحماية من المادة، مو من الغمق ولا السعر. |
| 26-30 | close | check wear: protection fades and scratches reduce it | والخدوش والزمن يقللون الحماية. |

- **Evidence record:**
  - Sunglasses labelled UV400 or 100% UV protect regardless of fanciness or darkness - [S88, verified] quote: "As long as they are labeled with “UV400” or “100% UV protection”"; scope: AAO 2022; no brands used
  - 100% UV = transmittance below 0.05% up to 380 nm - [S90, verified, script-only] quote: "transmittance at wavelengths up to 380 nm is less than 0.05%"; scope: ISO 12312-1:2022 as summarised by Swiss BAG
- **Risk:** None major
- **Confidence:** مرجح

## Finalist C - M02 / `uv-contacts-not-enough` / payoff `need-sunglasses-too`

- **Hook:** عدسة UV لاصقة لحالها ما تكفي. عيونك وجلدك حواليها مكشوفين.
- **Gloss:** A UV contact lens alone isn't enough: your eye surface and the skin around it stay exposed.
- **Curiosity gap:** What stays unprotected? (carried by: text)
- **First frame:** Eye close-up with the covered cornea highlighted and the rest shaded; text `الباقي؟`
- **Continuation:** Coverage
- **Payoff:** What contacts don't cover
- **Retention plan:**
