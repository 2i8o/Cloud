# B01 - Pinhole demo  (v1.1.0)

**Brief:** Show that a small hole in a card makes blurred text readable. Phone, printed line, card.  
**Audience:** Iraqi adults 18-40, many wear glasses | **Platform:** YouTube Short | **Duration:** 30 s

**Idea verdict:** PROCEED. Angle a standard explainer lacks: the size trade-off (why ~1.2 mm, not smaller) and where the clue fails. Confidence: مرجح.

**Recommendation:** A - clearest visual with an honest control; later finalists carry the size and limits stories

## Finalist A - M11 / `pinhole-aperture` / payoff `aperture-shrinks-blur`

- **Hook:** نفس السطر. نفس الكاميرا. شنو تغيّر؟
- **Gloss:** Same line, same camera. What changed?
- **Curiosity gap:** What did the card with a hole change? (carried by: frame)
- **First frame:** Defocused printed line; black card with a ~1 mm hole enters from the right; the line becomes readable (optics demo, labelled)
- **Continuation:** Control: the same card without a hole changes nothing
- **Payoff:** Mechanism: smaller aperture, smaller blur spots; costs light
- **Retention plan:**

| time (s) | purpose | new information | draft spoken line |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0-3 | hook + reveal | line sharpens when the holed card enters | نفس السطر. نفس الكاميرا. شنو تغيّر؟ |
| 3-8 | control | the same card without a hole changes nothing; the hole is the variable | الورقة بدون ثقب ما تسوي شي. الثقب هو الفرق. |
| 8-17 | mechanism | each object point becomes a blur spot; a narrow aperture makes the spot smaller | كل نقطة تصير بقعة مشوشة، والثقب يضيّق الشعاع فتصغر البقعة. |
| 17-24 | cost | less light reaches the sensor/eye, so the image is dimmer | وتدفع ثمنها: ضوء أقل، فالصورة تصير أغمق. |
| 24-30 | limit + re-hook | this is an optics demo not an eye test; clinics use a specific size | هذا شرح بصريات مو فحص نظر. وبالعيادة يستعملون حجم معيّن — ليش؟ |

- **Evidence record:**
  - A small aperture can make blur from a focusing error look sharper - [S45, verified] quote: "people with a refractive error, such as myopia, can usually see better with the pinhole"; scope: clinical pinhole test; refractive error; shown here with a defocused camera as an optics analogy
  - Decreasing pupil size increases depth of focus - [S46, verified] quote: "Decreasing pupil size increases the depth of focus"; scope: StatPearls pupil-size chapter; optics principle
- **Risk:** A camera's defocus is an analogy, not the viewer's eye
- **Confidence:** مرجح

## Finalist B - M05 / `pinhole-size-tradeoff` / payoff `why-not-smaller`

- **Hook:** تصغّر ثقب الورقة تشوف أحدّ… وتصغّره أكثر يصير أسوأ. ليش؟
- **Gloss:** Make the hole smaller and you see sharper... smaller still and it gets worse. Why?
- **Curiosity gap:** Why would a smaller hole be worse? (carried by: voice)
- **First frame:** Three cards with holes of decreasing size photographed through the same defocused lens; the smallest is dark and soft
- **Continuation:** Diffraction and brightness
- **Payoff:** Why ~1.2 mm is the working size
- **Retention plan:**

| time (s) | purpose | new information | draft spoken line |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0-4 | hook + contrast | a middle hole is sharp; a tiny hole is darker and softer | تصغّر ثقب الورقة تشوف أحدّ… وتصغّره أكثر يصير أسوأ. ليش؟ |
| 4-12 | evidence of the effect | the smallest hole looks worse than the middle one on camera | شوف: الثقب الأصغر أغمق وأنعم من الوسط. |
| 12-21 | reason | below about 0.75 mm diffraction blurs the image again and the view darkens | تحت حدود ٠.٧٥ ملم، الضوء ينثني على الحافة فالصورة تتشوّش مرة ثانية. |
| 21-27 | working size | clinical occluders use about 1.2 mm | عيادات النظر تستقر على حوالي ١.٢ ملم. |
| 27-30 | close | optics is a trade-off | بالبصريات دايماً اكو ثمن لكل تحسين. |

- **Evidence record:**
  - Clinical pinhole is about 1.2 mm; smaller apertures add diffraction blur - [S65, verified] quote: "if the pinhole aperture is smaller than 1.2 millimeters, the blurring effects of diffraction"; scope: clinical pinhole for refractive-error assessment; Whitney 2009 tested 0.5-1.5 mm; Kim 2014 reports blur below ~0.75 mm
  - Apertures below ~0.75 mm cause considerable diffraction blurring - [S66, verified, script-only] quote: "aperture size smaller than 0.75 mm causes considerable diffraction blurring"; scope: IOVS 2014 pinhole-glasses study discussion
- **Risk:** Hard to film the three sizes legibly (marked UNFILMABLE if the smallest hole is not visible on camera)
- **Confidence:** مرجح

## Finalist C - M16 / `pinhole-clue-limits` / payoff `clinical-clue-and-its-limits`

- **Hook:** نفس الثقب يحسّن النظر عند ناس، ويخليه أسوأ عند ناس آخرين.
- **Gloss:** The same pinhole improves sight for some people and makes it worse for others.
- **Curiosity gap:** Why does the same hole help some people and not others? (carried by: text)
- **First frame:** Clinician-style hand holding a pinhole occluder in front of an eye-chart line, then a second line that does not improve; on-screen text `ليش؟`
- **Continuation:** If blur is from focusing error it improves; some eye conditions do not
- **Payoff:** Clue vs diagnosis
- **Retention plan:**

| time (s) | purpose | new information | draft spoken line |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0-4 | hook | the same device gives opposite results | نفس الثقب يحسّن النظر عند ناس، ويخليه أسوأ عند ناس آخرين. |
| 4-12 | case 1 | with a focusing error, acuity usually improves through the hole | إذا التشويش من التركيز، الثقب يساعد. |
| 12-20 | case 2 | with macular disease or lens opacities the pinhole may not help or may worsen | وإذا اكو مشكلة بالشبكية أو العدسة، ممكن ما يساعد أو يخرّب. |
| 20-27 | why clinicians use it | the result is a clue under controlled conditions that guides the next test | الدكتور يستعمله كمؤشر، وبعدها يكمّل الفحص. |
| 27-30 | close (not a self-test) | a home attempt cannot tell you which case you are | وبالبيت ما تگدر تعرف أنت أي حالة. الفحص هو الجواب. |

- **Evidence record:**
  - Through a pinhole, vision improves with refractive error and may be unchanged or worse with macular disease - [S68, verified] quote: "Worse vision might indicate macular diseases or lens opacities"; scope: pinhole test discussion in a keratoconus paper; WHO uses it in rapid surveys; not accurate for estimating post-refraction acuity
  - Individuals with macular disease may have similar or even reduced acuity through a pinhole - [S65, verified] quote: "may have similar or even reduced acuity through a pinhole"; scope: clinical teaching document; macular and other central-vision disease
- **Risk:** Could be misread as a self-test; closing beat must hold
- **Confidence:** مرجح
