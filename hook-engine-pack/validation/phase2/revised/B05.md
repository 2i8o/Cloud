- **Curiosity gap:** Why that ratio? (carried by: voice)

- **Continuation:** Critical detail = 1/5 height
- **Payoff:** Geometry ties to one arcminute

- **Hook:** ليش فحص النظر يصير على ٦ أمتار؟
- **Gloss:** Why is the eye test done at 6 metres?
- **Curiosity gap:** Why 6 m and not 3? (carried by: voice)
- **First frame:** Tape measure from chart to chair labelled 6 m
- **Continuation:** Accommodation relaxed
- **Payoff:** The physical reason for the distance
- **Retention plan:**

| time (s) | purpose | new information | draft spoken line |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0-3 | hook | poses the question: Why 6 m and not 3? (carried by voice) | ليش فحص النظر يصير على ٦ أمتار؟ |
| 3-10 | tape measure | the chair is 6 m from the chart | الكرسي على ٦ أمتار بالضبط. |
| 10-18 | reason | looking far relaxes the focusing muscle so the eye is tested without extra focusing | لأن النظر البعيد يريّح عضلة التركيز، فنفحص العين من غير تركيز زايد. |
| 18-23 | same in feet | 20 feet is the same distance | وبالقدم هي ٢٠ قدم — ولهذا ٢٠/٢٠. |
| 23-25 | close | clinics without space use mirrors | والعيادات الصغيرة تستعمل مراية. |

- **Evidence record:**
