# B02 - Why eyes burn after reels  (v1.1.0)

**Brief:** Explain why eyes feel tired or burning after long scrolling without scaring people or pushing glasses.  
**Audience:** Iraqi 18-35 heavy phone users | **Platform:** Instagram Reel | **Duration:** 25 s

**Idea verdict:** PROCEED. Angle: let the viewer observe their own blinking (participatory) and keep the blue-light myth as a separate hook. Confidence: مرجح.

**Recommendation:** B - participatory and self-evidencing; A and C carry different takeaways

## Finalist A - M02 / `blue-light-not-cause` / payoff `cause-is-use-not-light`

- **Hook:** ليش عيونك تحرگ بعد ريلز… ومو بسبب الضوء الأزرق؟
- **Gloss:** Why do your eyes burn after reels - and it is not the blue light?
- **Curiosity gap:** If not blue light, then what? (carried by: voice)
- **First frame:** Phone in dark room with blue-light filter toggled; on-screen `ضوء أزرق؟`
- **Continuation:** AAO attribution on screen
- **Payoff:** Cause is how we use screens (blinking), not blue light
- **Retention plan:**

| time (s) | purpose | new information | draft spoken line |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0-3 | hook | negates the popular cause | ليش عيونك تحرگ بعد ريلز… ومو بسبب الضوء الأزرق؟ |
| 3-9 | authority | AAO: digital eye discomfort is not caused by blue light | الأكاديمية الأمريكية لطب العيون تگول: التعب مو من الضوء الأزرق. |
| 9-17 | real driver | we blink less when staring at screens, so the surface dries | السبب انك ترمش أقل لما تطالع الشاشة، فالسطح يناشف. |
| 17-22 | what helps | breaks and looking away; artificial tears per AAO | استراحات، وطالع بعيد، ودموع اصطناعية إذا ناشفة. |
| 22-25 | close | discomfort is temporary but persistent symptoms deserve an exam | وإذا استمر التعب، راجع مختص نظر. |

- **Evidence record:**
  - AAO: digital eye discomfort is not caused by blue light - [S32, verified] quote: "Digital eye discomfort is not caused by blue light"; scope: AAO digital devices page, Dec 2025; screen use in adults
  - We blink less when staring at screens - [S32, verified, script-only] quote: "blink rate can be cut in half when staring at screens"; scope: AAO page; population-level statement
- **Risk:** Same domain as B; payoffs differ (cause vs self-observation)
- **Confidence:** مرجح

## Finalist B - M10 / `blink-self-count` / payoff `your-own-count-vs-normal`

- **Hook:** طالع بوجه واحد يتفرج ريلز دقيقة… وعدّ كم مرة يرمش.
- **Gloss:** Watch someone scroll reels for a minute and count how often they blink.
- **Curiosity gap:** Is my number low? (carried by: frame)
- **First frame:** Close-up of a friend scrolling; blink counter overlay starting at 0
- **Continuation:** Compare your count with the usual rate
- **Payoff:** What a 'low' count means and does not mean
- **Retention plan:**

| time (s) | purpose | new information | draft spoken line |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0-3 | hook + task | viewer is asked to count | طالع بوجه واحد يتفرج ريلز دقيقة… وعدّ كم مرة يرمش. |
| 3-14 | observe | a minute of scrolling with the counter visible | (ريلز بدقيقة — العدّاد يشتغل) |
| 14-19 | benchmark | normal blink rate is around 15 per minute and can be cut in half at screens | الطبيعي حوالي ١٥ بالدقيقة، وبالشاشة ينزل للنص تقريباً. |
| 19-23 | meaning | counting is informal: attending to your own blinking changes it, and this is not a measurement | هذا مو قياس دقيق، بس يعطيك فكرة ليش تحرگ. |
| 23-25 | close | one habit to try | جرّب ترمش عمداً بعد كل شاشة، وشوف الفرق. |

- **Evidence record:**
  - Normal blink rate is around 15 per minute and can be cut in half at screens - [S32, verified] quote: "Normally, we blink around 15 times per minute"; scope: AAO digital devices page; adults; 15 blinks per minute normal
- **Risk:** A self-count is imprecise; must not be framed as a test
- **Confidence:** مرجح

## Finalist C - M08 / `twenty-twenty-twenty-evidence` / payoff `what-is-recommended-vs-proven`

- **Hook:** قاعدة ٢٠-٢٠-٢٠: تنفع لو بس نصيحة؟
- **Gloss:** The 20-20-20 rule: does it work or is it just advice?
- **Curiosity gap:** Is there evidence behind the rule? (carried by: voice)
- **First frame:** Timer overlay while someone looks out of a window
- **Continuation:** AAO recommends it; efficacy evidence needs retrieval
- **Payoff:** What the rule is, who recommends it, what is not shown
- **Retention plan:**

| time (s) | purpose | new information | draft spoken line |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0-4 | hook | asks about evidence | قاعدة ٢٠-٢٠-٢٠: تنفع لو بس نصيحة؟ |
| 4-11 | rule | every 20 minutes look at something 20 feet away for 20 seconds | كل ٢٠ دقيقة، طالع شي بعيد ٢٠ قدم لمدة ٢٠ ثانية. |
| 11-18 | status | AAO recommends it; efficacy trials must be sourced before publishing (RESEARCH) | الأكاديمية توصي بيها، وأنا راح أجيب الدراسات قبل لا أگول إنها مثبتة. |
| 18-23 | fallback | other AAO tips that do not depend on the rule: blink, distance, lighting | وتوصيات ثانية: رمش، مسافة ٢٥ إنج، وإضاءة مناسبة. |
| 23-25 | close | tell the viewer what is proven and what is advice | النصيحة موجودة، والإثبات نشوفه بالمصدر. |

- **Evidence record:**
  - AAO recommends the 20-20-20 rule - [S33, verified] quote: "Take regular breaks using the “20-20-20” rule"; scope: AAO 2021; screen users; recommendation, efficacy not reviewed
- **Risk:** Payoff incomplete until the efficacy evidence is retrieved (marked RESEARCH)
- **Confidence:** تخمين
