## Finalist A - M05 / `two-curvatures` / payoff `blur-at-all-distances`

- **Hook:** ليش قرنية بشكل كرة الرگبي تخلي النظر مشوّش قريب وبعيد؟
- **Gloss:** Why does a rugby-ball-shaped cornea blur both near and far?
- **Curiosity gap:** Why both near and far? (carried by: voice)
- **First frame:** A basketball and a rugby ball rotating with light rays overlaid
- **Continuation:** Mismatched curvature
- **Payoff:** Mechanism of blur at all distances
- **Retention plan:**

| time (s) | purpose | new information | draft spoken line |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0-4 | hook | poses the question: Why both near and far? (carried by voice) | ليش قرنية بشكل كرة الرگبي تخلي النظر مشوّش قريب وبعيد؟ |
| 4-12 | normal eye | round surfaces bend light equally to one focus | السطح المدور يجمع الضوء بنقطة وحدة. |
| 12-21 | astigmatism | two curves bend light differently; two focus points | منحنيين مختلفين يعطون نقطتين تركيز. |
| 21-27 | correction | glasses with a cylindrical lens compensate | عدسة اسطوانية تعوّض. |
| 27-30 | close | symptoms need an exam to confirm | والأعراض لحالها ما تأكّد، الفحص يأكّد. |

- **Evidence record:**
  - Astigmatism is an imperfection in the curvature of the cornea or lens; near and far vision are blurry - [S84, verified] quote: "With either type of astigmatism, near and far vision is blurry"; scope: AAO 2025; analogy: football vs basketball
- **Risk:** Analogy invites self-diagnosis if not framed; closing beat holds
- **Confidence:** مرجح

- **Payoff:** Why exams, not symptoms, find it
