# Idea gate - scoring anchors and examples

Scale per factor: **0** = absent/negative, **1** = partial, **2** = clearly present. Total 0-28. Thresholds are starting defaults, not validated cut-offs.

| Factor | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Audience demand | no sign anyone asks | adjacent searches/questions | repeated, verifiable questions/comments (label verified vs inferred) |
| Relevance to target viewer | affects nobody specific | affects a group indirectly | viewer can recognise it in their own life in 1 s |
| Novelty | everyone's seen it | familiar topic, new angle | mechanism/fact most viewers have not met |
| Insight strength | restates common knowledge | reframes | overturns or explains something viewers believed |
| Curiosity-gap potential | no question arises | vague question | one specific question the video can answer |
| Demonstrability | words only | diagram/animation | physical demo, real device, or on-screen evidence |
| Scientific credibility | claim unsupported | plausible, source not yet read | verified by primary/official source |
| Practical usefulness | none | mild | changes what the viewer does or understands tomorrow |
| Significance | trivial | mildly interesting | intellectually or personally meaningful, without fear |
| Payoff deliverable | needs distortion | partial | fully answerable in the duration |
| Fits 20-45 s | needs 3 minutes | tight | comfortable |
| Distinctiveness vs saturated content (inverse) | identical to hundreds | common format | recognisably different |
| Opening image | none | generic (eye close-up) | specific, readable object/event in frame 1 |
| Resources | cannot be made | stock/simulation only | creator can film/build it |

Decision defaults: **>= 20** and no 0 on credibility/payoff/relevance -> PROCEED. **14-19** -> RE-ANGLE or RESEARCH (whichever factor is the 0/1 bottleneck). **< 14**, or a 0 on credibility or payoff that cannot be repaired -> REJECT or RE-ANGLE. Any factual-integrity failure overrides the total.

## Worked diagnoses (optics)

**Idea: "20/20 vision means good eyesight."** X/Y/Z/D: X = "20/20 = perfect", Y = "it is the average-normal reference, not perfect; many see better", Z = Snellen fraction compares you with a reference distance, D = chart. Verdict **RE-ANGLE**: true and sourced (AAO "What does 20/20 vision mean?"), but saturated and the demo is a chart anyone has seen. Stronger angles: (a) why the letters on the chart have those exact sizes (1 arcminute detail), (b) a pinhole making a blurred line readable (mechanism visible in 5 s; sources: StatPearls pupil-size chapter, Community Eye Health pinhole test), (c) what the chart cannot tell you (acuity != eye health) - needs care, see scientific rules.

**Idea: "Phone screens are destroying your eyes."** Insight test fails: Y is not supported (AAO: no evidence screens cause permanent damage; discomfort is mostly reduced blinking). Fear is the only engine. **REJECT** as framed; **RE-ANGLE** to "why your eyes burn after a long reel: you blink about half as often" (verify the exact numbers against AAO before use).

**Idea: "Blue-light glasses do nothing."** Over-claim in the other direction. Evidence: AAO does not recommend them for eye strain; a 2023 narrative review (PMC9938358) found no consistent evidence of benefit for eye strain or disease prevention, while noting long-term cumulative exposure effects are unknown. "Do nothing" ignores sleep/circadian evidence discussions and product variety. **RE-ANGLE**: "what the glasses were tested to do, and what the evidence says" - keep verbs at the evidence level.

**Idea: "Wearing glasses makes your eyes dependent."** Evidence exists (AAO myth 14: glasses do not worsen vision or cause disease). Strong myth-correction, relevant to Iraqi families, fits 25 s. But note: needs care - myopia control lenses *do* change progression in children (D'Andrea 2025 meta-analysis of 23 RCTs), so the hook must not imply lenses never influence the eye. **PROCEED with scoped claim.**

**Idea: "Eye exercises fix myopia."** AAO myth 5 says exercises will not improve vision except convergence insufficiency. Correcting the myth is fine; **REJECT** any framing that promotes exercises as treatment.
