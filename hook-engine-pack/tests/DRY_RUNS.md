# Dry runs (self-executed, self-graded)

**Limits, stated first.** These are condensed responses the author (a language model) wrote by following the skills' procedures on eight of the 38 cases. They were graded by the same author against each case's *hard failure conditions* and quality criteria. That is a consistency check, not an independent evaluation: it can reveal contradictions in the instructions (it did - see the end) but cannot show that the skills generalise. No live harness ran the installed skills against fresh prompts. Iraqi lines are drafts.

Response style follows hook-engine: uncomfortable fact first, no filler, confidence labels.

### T03 - "make 'the eyes are the most important organ' dramatic"
> **الفكرة نفسها ضعيفة، مو الصياغة.** "العين أهم عضو" ما تجاوب على X/Y/Z/D: ما اكو اعتقاد خاطئ يتصحح ولا شي يتشاف. أي هوك درامي راح يغطي على هذا الضعف. **القرار: RE-ANGLE.** (مرجح)
> زوايا بديلة: (1) ثقب بحجم دبوس يوضّح السطر المشوّش - آلية تتشاف بـ5 ثواني (S45, S46). (2) 20/20 مو نظر مثالي (S44). (3) 20 ثانية بعيد عن الشاشة: شنو توصي به AAO وشنو ما ثبت (S33). أي وحدة تگدر تصوّرها؟
Grade: no dramatic hook written ✔; weak-idea statement in line 1 ✔; angles specific and demonstrable ✔. **Pass.**

Grade: not approved ✔; rewrite names an object ✔; no new generic question ✔. **Pass.**

## Defects the dry runs exposed (all fixed, tests re-run: 47/47)
1. **Contradiction in T36/constraint 4:** the case demanded refusing a product recommendation even if requested, but the permanent constraint allows it on explicit request. Fixed the case, the engine text, and the linter (`brief.product_mention_requested`), with a test.
2. **T09 used a verdict name that does not exist** (`PROCEED_AFTER_RESEARCH`). Fixed to RESEARCH and added a precedence rule (REJECT > RESEARCH > RE-ANGLE > PROCEED) to hook-idea-diagnosis, which T26 needed.
3. **OptiNest in a brief** (T38) had no explicit handling: hook-engine now says to note it in one line and proceed with the creator's name.
4. The analytics script's own disclaimer tripped the causal-language detector ("caused"); reworded.
5. Arabic registers: the first draft of the dialect table contained an unusable "want" row; removed.
